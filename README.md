ChimeX is a lightweight, elegant desktop application designed to provide gentle, regular sound reminders throughout your day. It helps you stay focused, take regular breaks, maintain healthy posture, and stay conscious of passing time without harsh disruptions.

## How it works

- **Subtle Reminders:** Rings at the interval you choose (every 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes).
- **Curated Sound Library:** A collection of soothing acoustic chimes designed to alert without startling.
- **Working Hours:** Set your active schedule so ChimeX stays quiet when you are off the clock.
- **Unobtrusive:** Lives in your macOS menu bar or Windows/Linux system tray, consuming minimal RAM and CPU.
- **Bilingual:** Automatically adapts to your system language (French / English).
- **Auto-Updates:** Automatically checks for and installs signed updates in the background.
